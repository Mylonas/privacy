Privacy Policy for MeldPDF

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · com.mikmy.meldpdf

The short version

MeldPDF does not ask for an account, a login, or any personal details. Every PDF operation — merging, splitting, compressing, converting, signing and the rest — runs entirely on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to any server.

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some information described below.

Your files

Files you open are read only to perform the operation you asked for. The result is saved only where you choose to save or share it. The app has no analytics of its own.

Advertising

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID and related device information, under Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob & Privacy
  • Google's Privacy Policy

In the EEA and UK, the app asks for consent (Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy options in the app.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android: Settings -> Privacy -> Ads.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch ads. Every PDF tool works offline.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top.
