Privacy Policy for Fuse

Last updated: 4 August 2026 · com.mikmy.fuse

The short version

Fuse does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. It does not collect anything you type. The only things it stores are your scores, and they stay on your phone.

The app does show ads, and the advertising network that serves them collects some information. That is described below.

What the game itself stores

  • your best score
  • the biggest tier you have fused
  • how many runs you have played

This is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Fuse shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • general device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • coarse, IP-derived location
  • your interactions with the ads themselves

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it collects and how it is used is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Personalised ads — if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time it runs, and you can change that answer later from the same privacy options dialog.

Children

Fuse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback during play.
