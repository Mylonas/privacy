Privacy Policy for ClearScan

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · com.blackfalcon.clearscan

ClearScan ("the app") is developed by Mylonas. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the app itself collects

Nothing that leaves your device by default. ClearScan has no account, no login, and no sign-up. Your scanned documents, their recognized text (OCR), and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Text recognition runs on-device (via Google ML Kit) — your document images and their text are never sent to us or to any server we control.

You choose when anything leaves the device: tapping Share, Send as fax, Save to…, Export all (ZIP), or Back up to a folder sends a document (or all of them) to an app or location you pick. "Back up to a folder" writes PDFs into a folder you choose via Android's file picker — if you pick a folder backed by Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or similar, that app's own account and policies apply to what you put there; ClearScan itself has no cloud account.

Advertising

The free tier shows a single small banner ad via Google AdMob, limited to the document list screen (never during scanning, editing, or sharing). To serve it, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID and related device information. This is standard for ad-supported apps and governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob & Privacy

In the EEA and UK, the app shows a consent request (Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalised ads, as required by law.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only whether the purchase was made (to unlock the feature); we never receive your payment details.

Feedback

If you use "Send feedback", it opens your email app with the app version and device model pre-filled, to help us debug — never any document content. You choose whether to send it.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.
