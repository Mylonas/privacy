Privacy Policy for EmberDepth

Last updated: 4 August 2026 · com.mikmy.emberdepth

The short version

EmberDepth does not ask for your name, your email, or any account. It does not collect anything you type. Everything the game stores — your heroes, gear, progress and settings — stays on your phone.

The app shows optional rewarded ads and offers a one-time purchase to remove them. The advertising network and the payment platform each collect some information, described below.

What the game itself stores

  • hero levels and formations
  • gear, materials and forge state
  • Rebirth progress and Ember upgrades
  • achievements, lifetime statistics and your daily reward streak
  • tutorial progress and sound settings

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

EmberDepth shows optional rewarded ads supplied by Google AdMob. You choose when to watch them; they are never forced. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • general device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • coarse, IP-derived location
  • your interactions with the ads themselves

Google acts as an independent controller of that data. What it collects and how it is used is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID — Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Personalised ads — if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time it runs, and you can change that answer later from the same privacy options dialog.

In-app purchases

EmberDepth offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the transaction; the app never sees your payment details. Your purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on any device where you sign in with that account. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

EmberDepth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch ads and verify purchases. The game itself works offline.
